Questions

Good to know.

Can you mount a TV on concrete walls?

Yes — concrete is our home turf. Most Oʻahu condos and townhomes are poured concrete, so we carry masonry bits and anchors rated well beyond your TV’s weight.

Can you hide the cables inside the wall?

Usually, yes. We check for studs, fire blocks and outlets first. Where in-wall isn’t possible (like solid concrete), we use a slim paintable raceway so nothing dangles.

Are you a licensed contractor?

No, and we won’t pretend otherwise. Happy Max Handyman Service LLC works within Hawaii’s handyman exemption (HRS §444-2): jobs where labor and materials together come to $1,500 or less, that don’t need a building permit, and that aren’t electrical or plumbing work. TV mounting sits comfortably inside that. If a job needs new wiring, a new outlet behind the TV or any other licensed trade, we’ll say so up front and point you to someone who holds the right license.
