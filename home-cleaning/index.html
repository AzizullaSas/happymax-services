Questions

Good to know.

What’s the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular clean keeps a home tidy. A deep clean goes after the build-up: baseboards, grout, inside and behind appliances, cabinet fronts, switches and the corners a regular clean skips. It’s the right start for a new home, a move-out or a reset after a busy season.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. If you won’t be there, we’ll arrange access with you ahead of time.

How is the price set?

It depends on the size of the home, its condition and any add-ons. Tell us the details and you’ll get a quote before any work begins.
