What we do

Everything your car needs, nothing it doesn’t.

Hand Wash & Seal

A careful hand wash of paint, glass, wheels and tires, finished with a protective sealant.

Interior Deep Clean

Seats, carpets and mats vacuumed and shampooed; dash, console, vents and glass wiped down.

Paint Correction

Machine polishing that takes out swirls, light scratches and oxidation to bring back depth and gloss.

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting protection against UV, salt air and water spots — easier washes and a deeper shine.

Headlight Restoration

Cloudy, yellowed lenses polished clear and sealed, so you see — and are seen — at night.

Salt & Sand Detox

The beach-day aftermath gone: sand out of the carpets, salt film off the paint and wheels.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Our detailing is fully mobile — we come to your home or office anywhere on Oʻahu.

Where can I see packages and prices?

On our dedicated detailing site, happymaxautodetailing.com — pick a package and request a time in about a minute. Prefer to talk? Call or text (808) 452-0002.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the package and the car — from about an hour and a half for a quick shine to a day or two for ceramic coating.
