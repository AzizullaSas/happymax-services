What we do

Clean, protected and ready to cast off.

Wash-Down

A fresh-water wash of topsides, deck, hardware and glass to clear salt before it builds up.

Gelcoat Polish & Oxidation Removal

Chalky, faded gelcoat compounded and polished back to color and gloss.

Wax & Sealant

A protective layer against UV and salt that holds the shine longer and makes washing easier.

Teak Care

Gentle cleaning and brightening of teak decks and trim, with sealing on request.

Interior & Cabin Cleaning

Cabins, galley, heads, upholstery and windows cleaned top to bottom.

Stainless, Vinyl & Canvas

Rails, cleats and fittings polished; vinyl, canvas and clear panels cleaned.

How it works

Simple from first message to finished job.

  1. Tell us about your boat

    Length, type, where she’s kept and what needs attention. Photos help a lot.

  2. Get a quote

    We’ll give you a price and suggest a plan — a one-time detail or regular wash-downs.

  3. We come to the boat

    We work at the harbor or on the trailer, so you don’t lose a day on the water.

Questions

Good to know.

What kinds of boats do you detail?

Sailboats, center consoles, powerboats and yachts. If it floats and needs cleaning, ask us.

Can you work at my harbor?

In most cases, yes. Some harbors have their own rules for outside vendors — tell us where the boat is kept and we’ll confirm before booking.

Do you offer regular wash-downs?

Yes. Besides one-time details, we can set up a recurring schedule so salt never gets the chance to build up.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (808) 452-0002 with your boat’s length, type and location. A few photos help us price it accurately.
